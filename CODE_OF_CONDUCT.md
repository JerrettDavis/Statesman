# Code of conduct

Statesman welcomes technical disagreement and expects professional conduct. Discuss designs, guarantees, tradeoffs, and evidence rather than assigning motives or dismissing contributors. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when needed to protect the project. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
